Give the extracted folder to one lead engineering session or team. Do not launch conflicting writers in the same workspace. This brief authorizes implementation within existing development permissions, not purchases or production deployment.

```text
Take ownership of building Client Mode from this complete v1.3 handoff.
Read START_HERE.md, ENGINEERING_HANDOVER.md, DOCUMENT_WORKFLOW.md under docs,
and the eight numbered implementation plans with their dependencies.

I am the client. Accept my ordinary English, Taglish, screenshots and files.
Handle product requirements, research, UI/UX, engineering, tools, tests, review,
repair, technical writing and release preparation. Keep routine reporting quiet.
Ask only important business, access, privacy, spending or release questions.

Your AI engineers must handle the appropriate client technology stack; the
TypeScript toolkit does not limit them. Preserve existing business rules and
unrelated work. Investigate unfamiliar technologies using actual project
versions and first-party documentation. Verify native/model/data behavior
with suitable tools, not a browser demo as a substitute.

Implement the document workflow: PDF, Word, Excel/CSV, PowerPoint and images;
source-version citations and complete scope accounting; actual spreadsheet
calculation; original-preserving edits and reopened/rendered exports. Support
document-only requests without inventing a software run or Git requirement.
Do not execute macros or external document actions, hide unreadable content,
claim whole-file review from truncated input, or treat provider skills as
already configured in every host. Add the source-change and feedback paths.

Cover CTO/PM/PO/delivery/design/engineering/QA/security/reliability and analyst,
writer/editor/support responsibilities as needed. Use one lead, one active
writer per project and at most two bounded children; no permanent company
swarm, fake research, artificial meetings or unconfigured support promises.

Use official Claude/Codex interfaces and original local procedures, with
approved maintained application/document libraries. Superpowers methods may
help implementation when installed but are not shipped dependencies. Verify
installed capabilities, permissions and billing. Do not invent CLI flags.

Run the handoff's checks, then implement all task gates with real execution.
Use failing tests, minimal fixes, independent review and protected verification.
Keep worker claims separate from actual evidence, and source/code/artifact
identity tied to current requirements. A document or software writer cannot
change its own protected evaluator, approve itself or forge client answers.

Build both provider adapters, controller, quiet console, safe tools, durable
state, budget controls, document pipeline, protected software/document QA,
installer, operations and qualification. T24 is a base release rehearsal;
T33 owns complete v1.3 shipment after G1–G8. Do not stop at documentation,
scaffolds, source reading, or a plugin that merely loads.

Stay within approved permissions and budget, stop non-progress loops and
save resumable state. Ask before additional paid APIs, confidential external
uploads, legal commitments, outbound communication or public/production work.
A local process that stopped is not continuing in the background.

Deliver the actual installed launcher, checked example outputs and truthful
qualification/limits. Do not claim all-stack expertise, document fidelity,
client satisfaction, cost savings or production readiness without evidence.
```

Human engineers assign one integration lead plus responsible security/release and QA owners. People may cover multiple responsibilities, but writing and approval authority must remain separated where specified. The client is not asked to manage these assignments or author the technical records.
